# Supplementary Figure: the PALM model and the program2cell pipeline

Files: `figS_palm_model.svg` (vector), `figS_palm_model.png`, `figS_palm_model@2x.png`.
Generated by `scripts/experiments/supp_model_figure.py` from the implementation (`bayes/model.py`,
`bayes/twostage.py`), method freeze v2.

**Legend (draft).**

**(a) Generative model of PALM, as implemented.** Plate notation. Open circles are latent variables, shaded
circles observed data, double circles deterministic functions, small filled dots fixed inputs or plug-in
estimates, and dashed nodes an optional component that is switched off in freeze v2. A node inside a plate is
repeated over that plate's index: patients *i* (N), cell types *c* (C), programs *p* (P), genes *g* (G) and
covariates *k* (K, drawn where each covariate weight lives).
- *Composition.* Each patient's composition logits η<sub>ic</sub> depend on a baseline a<sub>c</sub> (zero-sum), a
  group shift γ<sub>c</sub>, covariate weights A<sub>kc</sub> and patient-level noise with scale s<sub>η</sub>; the
  read fractions are π<sub>i</sub> = softmax(η<sub>i</sub>).
- *Program activity.* δ<sub>icp</sub>, the shift of program *p* in cell type *c* of patient *i* on the log scale
  of the reference profile, depends on a baseline κ<sub>cp</sub> and a group effect β<sub>cp</sub> (each with a
  regularised horseshoe prior: global scale τ, local scales ℓ<sub>cp</sub>, slab c² = 1), covariate weights
  D<sub>kcp</sub>, a patient factor u<sub>ip</sub> shared by all cell types with scale λ<sub>p</sub>, and residual
  variation with scale σ<sub>c</sub>. With lineage grouping, κ, β, D and σ are shared within a lineage.
- *Counts.* Each cell type's profile in a patient is the atlas profile B<sub>cg</sub> shifted by
  Σ<sub>p</sub> δ<sub>icp</sub> G<sub>pg</sub> on the log scale and renormalised (softmax over genes); the expected count
  μ<sub>ig</sub> is the library size L<sub>i</sub> times the read-fraction-weighted mixture of these profiles. Counts
  are negative binomial with gene dispersion θ<sub>g</sub>, a moment estimate from the bulk shrunk towards the
  median (plug-in). The platform scale m<sub>g</sub> is available but off in freeze v2 (replaced by the optional
  per-gene offsets of stage 0).
- Read fractions convert to cell fractions by dividing by each type's RNA content r<sub>c</sub> (mean UMI per
  cell in the atlas) and renormalising.

**(b) How the model is fitted and used.** Stage 0 (optional, real bulk) removes a per-gene platform offset
estimated from the study bulk. The model is fitted in two stages: each patient's likelihood is summarised by a
Gaussian at its mode (A1), and NUTS samples the population parameters with every patient's latents integrated
out exactly (A2); patient-level η<sub>i</sub>, δ<sub>i</sub> are then drawn from their Gaussian conditionals. The
posterior draws of π pass forward and nothing feeds back into the model (a cut, as in modular Bayesian
inference); the model's own δ and β are not reported. Each patient's reads are split between cell types by
π<sub>ic</sub>B<sub>cg</sub> (B, reallocation); the split's attenuation is calibrated on thinned reference donors with a
known truth (C); spillover between cell types is undone across patients by the effects router (D: unmixing with
REML shrinkage and a cluster bootstrap, or calibrated reallocation, chosen by interval width with a per-type
guard), using a design check of about 10 cohorts simulated with the study's fractions and design. Readouts (E):
per-cell-type group effects β̂<sub>cp</sub> with 90% intervals and calls, contributions split into "more cells" and
"cells changed" (Shapley), the shared response, robustness to unmeasured confounding and the design limit. In the
benchmarks the Bayesian fit (A1–A2) was used for McFarland, Kang and Zhang, and PALM-lite (EM, no posterior)
for Tahoe, Parse and COMBAT.
